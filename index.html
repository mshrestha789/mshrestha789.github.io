Manish Man Shrestha

Embedded Systems & Secure IoT Engineer

I design and deploy low-power, secure, real-time IoT systems across agriculture and structural monitoring. I have 15+ years of experience spanning embedded firmware (C/C++), FreeRTOS, custom hardware design, and end-to-end telemetry pipelines. My PhD research at South Dakota State University focuses on secure IoT-based monitoring systems integrating modern cryptography including post-quantum algorithms (Kyber, Dilithium) and authenticated encryption (ChaCha20-Poly1305), with secure transmission using MQTT over TLS. I also build C#/.NET and ASP.NET tools for data retrieval, verification, decryption, and real-time visualization.
